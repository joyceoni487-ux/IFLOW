How to activate AI replies (one-time setup)

  1. Go to vercel.com → your iFlow project → Settings → Environment Variables
  2. Add: GROQ_API_KEY = your Groq key (starts with gsk_)
  3. Add: STORE_NAME = your store name
  4. Add: PRODUCTS_JSON = your product list (click Copy Products for WhatsApp below)
  5. Click Save → Vercel auto-redeploys in ~30 seconds
  6. Come back here and click ↻ Refresh on the status badge above

Cloud Backup

Export your data to Google Drive or download as CSV / JSON anytime

Google Drive: opens a sign-in popup, uploads a JSON snapshot of all your data to your Drive folder. CSV / JSON: instantly downloads files to your computer — great for opening in Excel or Sheets. All data lives locally; nothing syncs in the background.

Email Alerts

Receive email notifications for low stock, new stock additions, and sales events

🟢 Option A — Gmail API (recommended, sends automatically)

Paste your OAuth 2.0 Client ID from Google Cloud Console, then click Connect. Emails send silently in the background. Token is stored locally — never sent to any server.

○ Not connected

After clicking Connect, a Google sign-in popup will appear asking for permission to send email on your behalf. You only need to do this once.

🔵 Option B — EmailJS (requires free account at emailjs.com)

If neither is configured, alerts open your default email app pre-filled so you can click Send manually.
